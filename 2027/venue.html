The venue

A castle on a hilltop

ISWS 2027 takes place at the University Residential Centre of Bertinoro (CEUB), in the Rocca, the castle at the top of the medieval town of Bertinoro. The town lies in the hills of Romagna, midway between Forlì and Cesena.

The castle has been converted into a modern conference centre with computing facilities and Internet access, and the school's sessions are held there. The participation fee includes accommodation and meals.

The town

A town of hospitality

In the main square, Piazza della Libertà, stands the Column of Hospitality (Colonna delle Anella). According to tradition, a traveller who tied their horse to one of its rings became the guest of the family that owned that ring. Every September, Bertinoro still celebrates this tradition with its Festa dell'Ospitalità.

A museum inside the castle

Since 2005, the castle has also housed the Interreligious Museum, dedicated to Judaism, Christianity and Islam.

Travel

Getting there

Shuttles for participants

Shuttles will be available from Bologna Airport and from Bologna to Bertinoro, and also for the return trip. The schedule and more information will be made available to the students later.

Travelling on your own

From Bologna airport, Bertinoro is reached by monorail, train and taxi.

Step 1
Fly to Bologna

Bologna Guglielmo Marconi Airport (BLQ) is about 80 km from Bertinoro. Rimini airport, about 40 km away, has flights from some European capitals.

Step 2
Marconi Express to Bologna Centrale

The monorail connects the airport with Bologna Centrale railway station in about 7 minutes. Timetable and tickets

Step 3
Train to Forlì or Cesena

Take a train on the Bologna – Rimini line and get off at Forlì or Cesena. Trenitalia

Step 4
Taxi up the hill

The taxi stand is in front of the station.
Forlì: +39 0543 31111
Cesena: +39 0547 610710

Which station?

The venue advises against the Forlimpopoli-Bertinoro stop, which is only a minor station. Use Forlì or Cesena instead.

By bus

Start Romagna line 92 to Forlimpopoli, then line 121 to Bertinoro. See the venue's directions for details.

By car

A14 motorway: exit Forlì when coming from Bologna, or Cesena Nord when coming from Rimini, then follow the signs to Bertinoro.
